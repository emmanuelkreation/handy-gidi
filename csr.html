Community Social Responsibility

Empowering Communities Through Practical Skills.

At Handy-Gidi Furnitures, we believe that no one is too young to learn a valuable skill or discover their creative potential.

Our Commitment

Inspiring the next generation of creative minds.

As part of our annual Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiative, we organise a free, one-day practical training every 27th of May in celebration of Children’s Day.

With the theme “Not Too Young to Be a Furniture and Interior Decoration Expert,” the programme brings children and adults together to gain hands-on experience in furniture making, woodwork, decoration and interior design.

Beyond the training, our goal is to inspire creativity, encourage practical learning and introduce participants to skills that can create future opportunities.

“Not Too Young to Be a Furniture and Interior Decoration Expert”

Every 27th of May, our workshop opens its doors to the community. We believe that a successful future is built on practical skills, creativity, and the confidence to build something with your own hands.
