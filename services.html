Our Services

More than furniture. A complete service.

From consultation to installation, we handle everything. Across Abuja and nationwide.

What we offer

Four pillars. One promise.

01 · SHOWROOM

Visit our showroom. Or we come to you.

Come see the craft in person at our Wuye showroom. Sit on the sofas. Feel the wood. Open the drawers. Or, if it's easier, schedule a doorstep delivery and we'll bring a curated sample to your space.

Whichever works for you, the goal is the same: no guesswork, no regrets.

02 · CONSULTATION

Personalized solutions for your space.

Every brief is different. A CEO needs a commanding office table that arrives this week. A family needs a full living room they can grow into. A hotel needs 40 identical units, on-time, on-budget.

We listen first, recommend second, and quote honestly third.

03 · CRAFTSMANSHIP

Skilled hands. Superior quality. Every piece.

Every item is built in our Abuja workshop by artisans who've spent years perfecting their trade. Material selection, joinery, finishing, quality check, packaging.

No shortcuts. No outsourcing the craft.

04 · TURN-KEY

Complete spaces. Not just individual pieces.

Interior design, construction, and renovation. We handle everything from the first sketch to the final walkthrough. One team, one point of contact, one invoice.

For offices, homes, hotels and developers who want to furnish at scale.
