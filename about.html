How it started

It began with a single Factory. And a stubborn belief.

Handy-Gidi International Ltd started with one Factory in Abuja and a stubborn belief that Nigerians deserved furniture that matched the ambition of their homes and offices. Not imported. Not rushed. Not overpriced. Just beautifully made, built to last, and delivered ready to use.

Every piece that leaves our floor carries that belief with it.

About Us

Handi Gidi Furnitures – Transforming Spaces, Creating Luxury.

Handi Gidi Furnitures is a furniture design and manufacturing company dedicated to transforming residential and commercial spaces through innovative designs, quality craftsmanship and functional furniture solutions. We specialise in the design, production, installation and furnishing of bespoke furniture tailored to meet our clients’ unique tastes, lifestyles and needs.

From elegant kitchen cabinets and modern wardrobes to luxurious walk-in closets, TV consoles, office furniture and complete interior furnishing, we bring creativity, precision and excellence to every project.

Our commitment goes beyond making furniture. We create beautiful, functional and comfortable spaces that reflect your personality and elevate your everyday living.

With a focus on quality materials, attention to detail, modern technology and skilled craftsmanship, we strive to deliver outstanding results from concept to completion.

At Handi Gidi Furnitures, we don’t just make furniture; we create spaces you’ll love to live and work in.
