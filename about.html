Our Story

The workshop behind the warmth.

A Nigerian brand, built on craft, delivering furniture that lasts.

How it started

It began with a single workshop. And a stubborn belief.

Handy-Gidi International Ltd started with one workshop in Abuja and a stubborn belief that Nigerians deserved furniture that matched the ambition of their homes and offices. Not imported. Not rushed. Not overpriced. Just beautifully made, built to last, and delivered ready to use.

Every piece that leaves our floor carries that belief with it.

What we do

We manufacture. We design. We install.

From single executive tables to complete residential and commercial fit-outs, Handy-Gidi operates as a true one-stop shop: interior design, furniture manufacturing, construction, and renovation. Under one roof.

Our clients include CEOs, families, hotels and developers who want scale without compromising on craft.

A note from leadership

We built Handy-Gidi on two beliefs: that Nigerians deserve furniture made to last, and that the next generation of craftspeople deserves a place to learn the trade.

Osasere leads strategy and operations across Handy-Gidi's furniture manufacturing arm and the Handy-Gidi Training Centre. One of the co-founders of the business, she's spent the last decade turning a small Abuja workshop into a full-service furniture, interior design and turn-key execution brand.
